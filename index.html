Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our massage therapy services.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a consultation to discuss your health history, goals, and preferences. We'll recommend the best treatment approach and ensure you're comfortable throughout the session.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with many insurance providers. Please call us to verify coverage for your specific plan and to discuss payment options.

How often should I get a massage?

Frequency depends on your individual needs and goals. For general wellness, monthly sessions are beneficial. For specific conditions or athletic performance, weekly or bi-weekly sessions may be recommended.

What's the difference between locations?

Both locations offer the same high-quality services. Our Blakeney location focuses on therapeutic treatments, while South Park offers a more spa-like atmosphere with additional amenities.
